## Representativeness gate
Unstratified, the harness under-reads casual (41% vs road 40% first-try only by coincidence of mixing; auto 0% vs road auto 14%) and engaged (50% vs road 100%). Stratified by mode the two agree: road attempts in the active window win 54 / 75 / 100% (light / casual / engaged) vs harness 84 / 82 / 98%; road attempts on Auto-Cast win 10 / 12 / 14% (idle / light / casual) vs harness 3 / 5 / 0%. The road's headline "first-try" number is therefore a mixture governed by which window the party arrives in: 0% of idle first attempts, 40% of light, 70% of casual and 100% of engaged land in an active window, far above the 0 / 8 / 17 / 25% time shares, because the party progresses much faster inside a window and the bot's windows sit at the top of every hour. That is a bot scheduling artifact: a player with irregular session timing would see something between the two mode rates. The harness passes the gate when read by mode, and the mode-stratified numbers are the better estimate of the boss.

## Failure mode (the answer to the pass)
1. The dominant failure is the Warlord's charge on Auto-Cast. In every profile's auto arm the charge lands 1.9-2.2 times per fight and kills a hero on essentially every hit (kills per fight 1.95-2.18, i.e. kill rate ~1.0 per hit), parries and interrupts are zero because Auto-Cast never reacts to a telegraph, and 88-100% of party damage comes from the boss. Auto losses are early: 26-81% of them wipe before the 50% summon with 44-62% boss HP left (median), the summon occurs in only 19-75% of auto fights, and adds are absent at the wipe.
2. In the active window the same parties neutralize the charge: 0.1-0.3 hits per fight, 0.3-0.7 interrupts and up to 0.17 parries per fight, and the loss shape flips to near-kills: 67-69% of light/casual active losses end with the boss under 20% HP, with two adds alive. The summon phase is reached in 99-100% of active fights and is survivable.
3. Arrival power does not explain outcomes: idle arrives with the highest power (14.5K, Lv 38) and wins least (22%), engaged arrives lower (13.4K) and wins most. Within-profile power terciles move win rate by 10-25 points; the mode moves it by 40-95 points. Arrival HP is 79-93% and charge ~50 in every profile; an HP<80% arrival is not consistently worse.
4. HP is not the lever: with the charge handled, parties reach the kill; without it they never reach 50%.

## Decision-rule mapping
"If arrival power or active-state differences explain most outcomes and the boss performs acceptably at comparable states: make no boss change and investigate arrival/training state." Active-state explains most outcomes and the boss performs acceptably when the party reacts to the charge. So I do not propose an HP, ATK or summon candidate. The state to investigate is not arrival or training; it is that Auto-Cast has no answer to a telegraphed charge, while the manual player has three (parry, interrupt, cleanse). Idle players never get the answer, light players get it 40% of the time by luck of timing.

## PROPOSED_EXPERIMENT (one lever, proposal only, nothing implemented)
Auto-Cast reactions: when an enemy is in the telegraph phase of a charge (`G.action.kind==='chargeM'`, phase `tele`) and a hero's ability is ready, Auto-Cast applies the same `reactTo()` the manual tap uses (Knight parries with Shield Wall, Rogue/Mage interrupt, Cleric cleanses), instead of casting on its own schedule. No new power: it spends the same charge the manual reaction spends, at manual timing. Control: current Auto-Cast. Candidate: Auto-Cast with reactions. QUICK DIAGNOSTIC in the boss harness first (same 40 snapshots, auto mode only, 400 fights, one process, minutes), then, if the auto win rate moves materially toward the active rate, the 30-run road diagnostic (seeds 1-10 x idle/light/casual) for propagation, since every charging enemy in the game is affected (Stillwater's Alpha does not charge; the Warlord, the Ashen bosses and the horde champions do).
EXPECTED_OUTCOMES: if the charge is the wall, idle's auto win rate at Ironvein rises from ~3% toward the active-window rate for its arrival power (~40%), charge kills per fight fall from ~2 toward ~0.3, and losses shift to the summon/near-kill shape; road idle first-try and attempts improve without touching the boss. If it does not move, the charge is not the whole story and an ATK-only (charge multiplier) candidate is the fallback, which I would then propose as the single boss lever.
Alternative the reviewer may prefer: an ATK-only candidate on the charge multiplier (2.5x) for the Warlord only. It fixes Ironvein for idle but leaves every other charge boss with the same Auto-Cast blind spot, and it also lowers the fight for active players who already handle the charge.
IMPLEMENTATION_RISK of the reaction rule: low-medium. It is a behavior change to Auto-Cast, so it touches horde fights and catacombs too; it must not double-fire with the normal auto cast in the same tick, and it should respect the existing "manual cast 30% stronger" bonus (auto reactions would be at auto power). It is simulator-measurable in both harnesses, save-compatible, and one boolean to gate for A/B.

## Caveats
Harness fights start from saved arrival state with statuses cleared and a fresh RNG per fight; per-snapshot outcomes vary widely, so the 95% CIs shown are the honest precision. The road "first-try" figures are n=10 per profile and, as shown, dominated by window timing. Summon "interrupted" is not measurable because the summon is threshold-triggered, not an action; reported as 0 by construction. Ward absorption is measured only on adds (the boss has no ward).
