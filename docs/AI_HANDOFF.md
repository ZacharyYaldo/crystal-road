ALTERNATIVE_HYPOTHESIS: "Stuck" on the Road means no forward progress, not no zone clear. The game already tracks the furthest fight reached per zone (`G.far`); a party crawling through Emberwaste sets a new furthest mark every few minutes, a party losing to a boss or to the Keep's ordinary fights does not. Proposed rule (`--stallRule far`, implemented as the bot default, with the review's literal rule kept available as `--stallRule clear`): stalled = at least three simulated hours with neither a zone clear nor a new furthest-fight mark in the current zone; everything else as the review specified (game eligibility, gain >= 15, Endless behaviour unchanged, stall clock and counters reset after a Shatter, no new retreat/training/combat rule). Under this rule the smoke Shatters land where a player is actually stuck: at the Amberfall boss after training, at the Ashen Approach boss, and inside the Keep (casual 31 at 47.1h, fight 24 of 36, Lv 59). Note the rule counts Auto Training time as stalled time (the party is away from the zone and its furthest mark does not move), which matches the player's experience of being stuck at a boss and is the case the Keep question needs.
