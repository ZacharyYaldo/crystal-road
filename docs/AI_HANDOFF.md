## Completion statement
Under the pass 32 completion rule the current tuning cycle is ready for human merge consideration: fresh seeds reproduce the tuned Road end to end with no severe regression and no material new hard wall. Scope of that statement: the values changed in this cycle (Warlord, Sand Tyrant, Hunter King, Hollow King and Core damage; the Core-only volley multiplier; post-Shatter enemy scaling; Ashen Keep level kept at 50; Auto Training kept at 1.0 / 4), validated on four bot profiles over 96 simulated hours with the accepted bot policies (far-mark Shatter rule, dust priority, literal ascension policy). Not covered: the Endless Road's power curve (documented in passes 27 and 31 as level-driven and monotone), the Grave Knight's charge for casual play, the ascension cost curve, tap and monetization systems, and real-player behaviour outside the four profiles.
