## What the loss telemetry says about the mechanism
The diagnosis (damage per action versus party HP) is confirmed rather than contradicted, but x1.35 is not enough of a cut to change the fight's shape:
- At x1.35 an ordinary Sand Tyrant hit still does about half a front hero's HP and 80% of a back hero's (hits-to-kill 1.9 / 1.2, from 1.3 / 0.8 at x1.9); the charge does 146% / 210% of a hero's HP (from 208% / 320%). For comparison the locked Warlord at natural idle arrival is 2.3 / 1.6 hits-to-kill and 121% / 166% on the charge.
- Every candidate loss reaches the summon (61/61 idle, 26/28 light, from 84% and 32%), and ends at 41-42% boss HP instead of 44-55%: the party now survives the opening but wipes in the two-sandorc phase, with three charge kills per loss. The enrage (25%) is reached in 8 of 61 idle losses.
- Wins end at 82-95% party HP with four survivors. So the fight is bimodal: either the charge sequence kills three heroes and the summon finishes the last, or the party gets past the charges and cruises. That is the Ironvein shape at x1.7, one step later.

## Recommendation (proposal only)
I do not recommend locking x1.35 as the finished Sand Tyrant value: it halves idle's attempts but leaves a 7-attempt, 3.2h wall with a 14% Auto-Cast rate, and light does not move. I also do not recommend a third per-boss cycle on the Sand Tyrant alone. The comparison above is the case for the reviewer's alternative: one scoped late-boss normalization experiment with a single design rule (front hero hits-to-kill at natural realistic arrival ~2.3, the locked Warlord's value), applied as ATK-only changes: Sand Tyrant x1.9 -> x1.1 and Hunter King x2.0 -> x1.0, Grave Knight left out because it is the intended Shatter wall and no realistic profile reaches it in a way the simulator can measure. Same experiment structure as this pass: paired seeds 31-40 x idle/light/casual x 36h, 30 + 30 runs, simulator override only, no production change; Ironvein rows must stay identical; Sand Tyrant judged on the pass 16 bands, Hunter King on light/casual only (idle does not reach Amberfall before 36h in either arm) with a note that its arrival states will shift when the Sand Tyrant changes upstream, so its rows are directional in this pass and would need the follow-up validation for a lock. Risk: two bosses move at once, so the Amberfall rows are conditional on the Emberwaste change; this is the price of not spending two more passes, and the reviewer can split it if that price is wrong. If the reviewer prefers to hold the per-boss discipline, the single-value alternative is Sand Tyrant x1.1 alone under the same design, and x1.35 can stand as an interim production value in the meantime since it is strictly better than x1.9 for idle and casual and neutral for light.
